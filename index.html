ABOUT THE PROJECT

About Project: Insight

Discover your leadership preferences, explore different management approaches and identify opportunities for personal and professional growth.

What is Project: Insight?

Project: Insight is an interactive self-assessment designed to help users reflect on how they approach leadership.

The assessment explores six management styles: Autocratic, Democratic, Laissez-faire, Transformational, Transactional and Coaching.

How does the assessment work?

The assessment contains 30 questions divided into two sections.

Section A - Scenario-based Questions

You will be presented 12 real-world scenarios. Select the response that best represents how you would handle each situation.

Section B - Management Behaviour Questions

You will be presented with 18 statements about general management behaviours. Rate your level of agreement using a five-point scale.

Questions and answer options are randomised where appropriate to reduce predictable patterns.

Understanding your results

Your responses in both sections contribute to a combined primary management profile.

Currently, both the scenario-based questions and management behaviour questions are equally weighted. This means that each section contributes 50% to each style's combined score. Though this is subject to change dependant on future research.

Your results include your leading management profile as well as closely matched styles (within 10%). It also displays potential strengths, challenges and personalised developmental advice.

You can also download your results for future reference.

Management Styles Library

The Management Styles Library is where you can find information about all six management approaches, including their strengths, potential challenges and development tips.

You can explore the library independently of the assessment.

About the developer

Project: Insight is a web development project created using HTML, CSS and JavaScript by Niara Kerr.

The project explores interactive questionnaire design, JavaScript-based scoring, dynamic interfaces and personalised feedback.

Important information

This assessment is intended for personal reflection. It is not a scientifically validated psychological assessment or a formal measure of leadership ability.

Management preferences may vary depending on the situation, level of experience and working environment.
